# resilient

## 释义
1. （人/经济）有韧性的，能迅速恢复的
2. （材料）有弹性的

## 例句
1. Children are often more **resilient** than adults expect.
2. The economy proved remarkably **resilient** to the crisis.

## 词根词缀
**re-**（回）+ **silire**（跳）→ 跳回去 → 恢复

## 记忆锚点
像弹簧：压下去能弹回来 → resilient
