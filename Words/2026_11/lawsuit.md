# lawsuit

`/ˈlɔːsuːt/` · n. · #六级

释义：诉讼；官司

例句：In recent months, class-action lawsuits have been filed against more than a dozen law schools, charging that students were deceived into enrolling by postgraduate employment figures that were vastly, and falsely, inflated.
译文：近几个月来，超过十二所法学院遭到集体诉讼，指控它们用被大幅夸大且虚假的研究生就业数据诱骗学生入学。

词形变化：
- lawsuit (n.)：诉讼；官司
- lawsuits (n.)：诉讼（复数）
- sue (v.)：起诉；控告

用法：
- file a lawsuit：提起诉讼
  例：The workers filed a lawsuit against the company.（工人们对公司提起诉讼。）
- a class-action lawsuit：集体诉讼
  例：The consumers brought a class-action lawsuit.（消费者提起了集体诉讼。）

词根词缀：law（法律）+ suit（诉讼）

记忆锚点：把争议交给法院解决 → 诉讼
