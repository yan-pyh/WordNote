# stockmarket

`/ˈstɒkˌmɑːkɪt/` · n. · #六级

释义：股票市场；股市

例句：Our calculations do not cover long-term potential costs and benefits, such as outlays on maintenance, the asset value of a home once a mortgage has been paid off, or the opportunity cost of investing in a deposit for a house rather than, say, the stockmarket.
译文：我们的计算没有涵盖长期潜在成本与收益，例如维护支出、房贷还清后的房屋资产价值，或把购房首付款用于投资而不是投入股市的机会成本。

词形变化：
- stockmarket (n.)：股票市场；股市
- stockmarkets (n.)：股票市场（复数）

用法：
- invest in the stockmarket：投资股市
  例：They invest in the stockmarket for the long term.（他们长期投资股市。）
- stockmarket crash：股市崩盘
  例：The stockmarket crash wiped out many savings.（股市崩盘使许多储蓄化为乌有。）

词根词缀：stock（股份）+ market（市场）

记忆锚点：买卖股票的市场 → 股市
