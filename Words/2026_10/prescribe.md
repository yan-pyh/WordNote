# prescribe

`/prɪˈskraɪb/` · v. · #六级

释义：开处方；规定；（prescription）处方；药方

例句：Nurse practitioners and physician assistants have been given responsibilities typically reserved for doctors, such as writing prescriptions.
译文：执业护士和医师助理已被赋予通常由医生承担的职责，例如开具处方。

词形变化：
- prescribe (v.)：开处方；规定
- prescription (n.)：处方；药方
- prescriptions (复数)

用法：
- prescribe medicine：开药
  例：The doctor prescribed medicine for the infection.（医生为感染开了药。）
- write a prescription：开处方
  例：She wrote a prescription for pain relief.（她开了一张止痛处方。）

词根词缀：pre-（预先）+ scrib（写）→ 预先写下用药或规则

记忆锚点：医生提前写下用药方案 → 开处方
